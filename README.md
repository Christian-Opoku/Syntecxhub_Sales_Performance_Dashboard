## 🔍 Dashboard Insights

### Sales Trends

The dashboard provides sales trends across different time periods, with
the current view showing quarterly performance:

-   **Q4:** \$587K
-   **Q3:** \$401K
-   **Q2:** \$317K
-   **Q1:** \$261K

The quarterly view shows a strong increase in sales toward Q4.

### 🌍 Region-wise Sales

Sales are compared across four regions:

-   **West:** \$522K
-   **East:** \$450K
-   **Central:** \$341K
-   **South:** \$252K

The **West region** records the highest sales, while the **South
region** records the lowest among the four regions.

### 🛍️ Category-wise Sales

The dashboard compares performance across major product categories:

-   **Office Supplies:** \$644K
-   **Technology:** \$471K
-   **Furniture:** \$452K

**Office Supplies** is the highest-performing category based on sales.

### 🔻 5 Lowest Performing Products

The dashboard includes a visual ranking of the five lowest-performing
products, helping identify products that may require further
investigation, pricing adjustments, marketing support, or inventory
review.

### 🔝 Top 5 Performing Products

The dashboard also identifies the five highest-performing products. The
leading product shown generates approximately **\$704K**, followed by
other high-performing products.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

This dashboard was created to:

-   Monitor overall sales and profit performance
-   Identify sales trends over time
-   Compare regional sales performance
-   Analyze category-level sales
-   Identify top-performing products
-   Identify low-performing products
-   Present business insights through an easy-to-understand visual
    interface
